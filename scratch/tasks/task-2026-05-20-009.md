## 1. Problem Analysis

### AS-IS

`AGENTS.md` Agent Coordination Workflow step 2 says:
> Module analysts (SD, MM, etc.) discuss the request based on research data.

The Business Analyst Selection table maps single-module trigger keywords. There is no rule for what happens when keywords from two modules appear together, or when the business process inherently spans modules.

### GAP

For a request like "Why isn't the FI document being created after SD billing?" both `sd-analyst` and `fi-analyst` are relevant, but:
- No rule specifies whether to activate both in parallel or sequentially.
- No rule specifies who owns the integrated PRD.
- No rule specifies how conflicting ACs from two analysts are reconciled.

### TO-BE Requirements

Add a "Cross-Module Integration Scenarios" section to `AGENTS.md` with:
1. A trigger rule: "If keywords match 2+ modules, activate both analysts in parallel."
2. A synthesis rule: "PM is the PRD owner; each analyst contributes their module's AC section."
3. A primary analyst designation rule: the module where the *symptom* originates is primary.
4. Standard cross-module scenario templates: SD-FI, MM-FI, SD-LE, PP-MM.

### Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] **AC-01**: `AGENTS.md` contains a "Cross-Module Integration" section with parallel activation rule.
- [ ] **AC-02**: The section specifies PRD ownership and AC synthesis procedure.
- [ ] **AC-03**: At least 4 standard cross-module scenario templates are documented.
- [ ] **AC-04**: The Business Analyst Selection table is updated with cross-module trigger examples.
